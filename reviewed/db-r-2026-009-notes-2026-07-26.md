# db-r-2026-009, what could not be substantiated

**Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Paper:** `frontend/src/app/modules/research/content/db-r-2026-009.md`
**Registry:** `frontend/src/app/modules/research/data/researchPapers.ts` (PAPER_009, newest-first)

This note records every figure or framing in the commissioning brief that the sources did
not support as stated, plus the sources I could not reach. It exists so a reader of the
paper can tell what was measured from what was asserted, and so the next person does not
re-derive the same corrections.

## 1. Brief claims that were corrected before printing

### 1.1 "the same afternoon it was adopted"

**Not substantiated.** The brief says contract v1.1 §12.4 invalidated the 25/25 G2 pack
"the same afternoon" it was adopted. Same *day* is substantiated and is what the paper
prints:

- `scc-v2/contracts/AMENDMENT-01-kernel-verification-regime-2026-07-26.md:3` is headed
  "Status: FULLY ADOPTED 2026-07-26".
- `scc-v2/reviewed/step3-g2-holdout-2026-07-26.md` is dated 2026-07-26.

No timestamp finer than the date appears in any artifact, and **no build date is recorded
for the voided pack at all**, only that the clauses were "introduced by AMENDMENT 01 *after*
it was built". §3.2 of the paper says "the day §12.4 was adopted".

### 1.2 "invalidated because it was Fable-built and opus5-authored"

**True but incomplete, and the omission matters.** The pack was voided on **two
independent grounds, either sufficient** (`step3-g2-holdout-2026-07-26.md` §0):

1. Same model family: unit built by Claude Fable 5 (commit `06c9eec`), pack `binding.json`
   records `"author_seat": "holdout-author-opus5-role"`.
2. **No adapter differential of any kind**, which §12.4 independently makes a void condition.

Printing it as a single-cause invalidation would misstate the document, and would also
overstate the family clause's demonstrated power. The paper prints both grounds, and §8.2
of the paper turns the confound into a falsification test: the clean same-family-with-CLEAN-
differential experiment has not been run.

### 1.3 §12.4 as the clause number at adoption time

**Partly a naming artifact.** AMENDMENT 01's own text numbers this clause **A3**; §12.4 is
where it lands once applied to the contract. Both labels are in circulation in the corpus.
The paper uses §12.4 because that is where the normative text now lives, and quotes it from
the contract rather than the amendment.

### 1.4 "the orchestrator seat must not be the default builder, a defect found and fixed today"

**Substantiated, but not where the brief pointed.** It is **not** in any of the ten
`scc-v2/reviewed/` documents; a repo-wide grep of `D:/claude/scc-v2` for that shape returns
zero matches. It is in code, `cortex_core/seating.py`, in the `_role_order` comment for
`RISK_HARD`, dated 2026-07-26. The paper quotes that comment directly (§3.5).

### 1.5 "a caller passing one argument where three were required"

**Substantiated, also not where the brief pointed.** Absent from every `reviewed/` document.
It is in the header comment of `scc-v2/.github/workflows/kernel-tests.yml`, which also
supplies "Four of fifteen recorded wiring failures were of exactly this shape".

**Caveat printed in the paper:** the fifteen-item wiring-failure register is referenced by
that header and **was not located as a standalone artifact**. The figure is attributed to
the header, not to a register.

### 1.6 "receipt component invoked zero times"

**Substantiated, from two places, neither in `reviewed/`.**
`scc-v2/scratchpad/holdout-115-opus5/PACK-115.md:58-60` ("`receipts.db` is 0 bytes after a
full run and the receipts component is invoked zero times") and the `kernel-tests.yml`
header. The paper cites PACK-115 as the primary.

### 1.7 "two agents editing one module" as an observed coordination hazard

**No recorded instance. Reported as absent in §4.3, deliberately.** A thorough search of
both repositories found: the risk named in design documents (2026-07-15, 2026-07-17); the
hazard actively routed around twice on 2026-07-26; and **no dated incident of an actual lost
write from concurrent same-file editing**. Nor any `.git/index.lock` contention event.

The brief's "an agent committing another's in-progress work" **is** substantiated, by the
2026-07-06 record.

### 1.8 "lost 5 of 9 rows"

**Substantiated as the source states it, with a caveat the paper carries.** The five is
**four clean losses (rows 1, 3, 8, 9) plus row 2, whose own "Who wins" cell reads "Split."**
Also carried: only rows 1, 4, 5 and 6 have dedicated positive controls; the conventional
column is **modelled from vendor documentation, not deployed**.

### 1.9 "23 effect routes, 1 reaches the authoriser"

**Substantiated verbatim.** One arithmetic note is printed in the paper because the source
prints it: 1 + 1 + 21 = 23 works because the one partial route is counted in both the
partial and the no-authoriser buckets.

### 1.10 "0/5 for stand-in detection"

**Substantiated at commit `d38b5d1`, and already overtaken.** `ops/wiring-oracle/` had zero
non-cache files at that pin; it now contains `kernel_spec.json`, `routes.py`, `spec.py`,
`standin.py`, `probes.py`. The paper prints the pinned measurement **and** the fact that it
has been overtaken (§6.3), rather than silently refreshing an unfavourable number.

### 1.11 "the cortex research doc argues cross-model review is not independent V&V"

**The opposite, and the paper corrects it.** `cortex/docs/research/adversarial-independent-
assessment-2026-07-26.md` *recommends* IV&V as the framing and treats independence as
partially purchasable. The negation lives in **contract §12.4 as corrected by AMENDMENT 02**,
and the chronology is worth having right: the research doc recommends the term, the term
enters the contract via AMENDMENT 01 (2026-07-26), an external adversarial review objects,
AMENDMENT 02 retracts it the same day. The freeze record's own line, quoted in the paper:
"this is not a presentation error propagating into the contract; it is a contract error
propagating outward, which is the more serious direction."

## 2. The corrected cold-start figures, and one nuance

The brief's figures are **all correct** against
`scc-v2/reviewed/evidence-ledger-coldstart-2026-07-26.md` §0: 11 raised, 11 reproduced,
10 distinct, 6 code defects, 4 contract ambiguities, 1 fixed (commit `1ea5d4f`).

The nuance the paper carries: "10 distinct" and "6 + 4" are **two different collapses**, not
a partition of 11 into 10. The *source assessment's* own split was 6 defects / 5 ambiguities
= 11; the ledger merged one finding into another to reach 10. A stricter reading that
compresses four findings sharing a root cause reaches **8 distinct**, and the ledger says it
"does not dispute" that reading. The paper prints the 8 as well.

Also printed, because the brief did not mention it and it materially weakens the headline:
**the brief supplied the attack surface.** Seven of eleven findings map onto one of ten
attack families the brief listed verbatim; **four of eleven** came from an unprompted angle.
And "we told them nothing" is false, the frozen contract was supplied.

   the definition of done its own adopted checkboxes replaced.
5. **All four `scc-v2/reviewed/` documents in the primary source set are untracked in git**

   says `kimi-k2.7-code`. The ledger records this as "not substantiated either way" and the
   paper does not print a kimi model version.

## 4. Sources I could not reach or chose not to cite

  earlier edition plus the IEEE Xplore record; the three-dimension independence definition
