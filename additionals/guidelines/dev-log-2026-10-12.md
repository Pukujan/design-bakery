## Summary

**TASK-DB-0084** promoted the cinematic-journey page (`frontend/version-2-app`) from
`/version-2` to the site homepage at `/`, moving the site shell SPA to
`/shell/index.html` and turning `/version-2` into a permanent 308 to `/`. It was merged
([#113](https://github.com/Pukujan/design-bakery/pull/113), `7c2343a`) and deployed by hand
on the Mac — and it was **not wanted**: the journey was always meant to be an *additive*
page, and the owner caught the swap in session. **TASK-DB-0085** reverts the routing and
build wiring to the pre-promotion (`0e91fd9`) state while keeping every piece of #113's
design work. `/` is the site shell SPA again; `/version-2` is the journey. The deploy is
owner-gated and has not run yet, so the live site still serves the promoted state.

Two things worth carrying forward:

- **The promotion's authorization was thin.** Its task file records the issue as *"None
  filed — owner direction in session ('finish the design and replace it')"*, and its own
  **Open owner decisions** section had left *"whether the retired shell homepage should
  stay reachable at some path"* unresolved. It merged and deployed anyway. A session
  paraphrase is not a substitute for the owner confirming a homepage replacement.
- **One genuine bug fix came out of it, and is kept.** `deploy.sh` checked out the
  requested ref but never re-exec'd, so a deploy ran the **outgoing** revision's `smoke()`
  against the **incoming** container. #113's own deploy log shows the trap firing: the
  `08:04:05Z` run failed `expected 200, got 308 for /version-2` and auto-rolled-back. The
  guarded `DEPLOY_REEXEC=1 exec "$SELF" "$@"` after the checkout stays — and it is exactly
  what makes *this* revert safe, since it flips `/version-2` from 308 back to 200.
