## Summary

Rebuilt the FOSSIL marketing case study and presentation around the corrected problem framing: FOSSIL is for human-led technical decisions made with AI assistance. The central gap is not that AI makes decisions; it is that transcripts, logs, tickets, commits, sources, citations and benchmarks preserve fragments of the work, while the authoritative reasoning chain that led the human to a decision is not preserved as durable lineage.

## Current public narrative (v4)

### 1. Corrected lead
- Lead: **“The evidence is everywhere. The reason is nowhere.”**
- Replaces agent-led framing with human-led technical work.
- AI is framed as an accelerator for research, comparison, testing and implementation — not as the decision owner.

### 2. Market gap
The page now explains that the artifacts usually survive:
- transcripts;
- commits and PRs;
- Jira tickets;
- benchmark output;
- source links and papers;
- review notes.

The missing layer is the preserved reasoning chain:
- which source mattered;
- which assumption changed;
- what failed;
- what was tried before;
- what the decision superseded;
- which later decisions depended on it;
- whether a later AI reconstruction is faithful to the original path.

### 3. Product promise
The public line is now:

> FOSSIL keeps the reasoning while the work is happening.

It is positioned as decision-lineage infrastructure for human-led work, not as better memory and not as autonomous-agent governance.

### 4. Sales demo
The demo question is:

> “Why did I choose the durable event core instead of SQLite?”

The answer shows current decision, what changed, what was tried, preserved evidence, open review, and why inspectable lineage matters.

### 5. Research posture
Research is used only to support the pain:
- rationale has many components;
- rationale appears in developer communication;
- resuming complex programming work costs context rebuilding;
- LLM-generated rationale is not the same thing as faithful historical provenance.

The main page avoids becoming an audit. The evidence ledger carries the caveats.
