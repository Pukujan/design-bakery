The real failure behind v0.2

The decision existed. The discovery did not.

PAM's build-vs-reuse revision came from a real shortcut in the Interview OS projectization flow. A self-authored decision artifact could look complete without proving that internal and external alternatives were actually examined. The new contract makes discovery itself inspectable.

01 · SEARCH

Record where you looked.

Internal assets and external candidates get their own discovery trail.

02 · READ

Use the real source.

A summary is weaker than documentation that was actually consulted.

03 · PROBE

Test the uncertain part.

When a reusable option is plausible, a bounded probe can turn opinion into evidence.

04 · DECIDE

Keep the tradeoff.

The reviewed disposition points back to the candidates and evidence that informed it.

A convincing plan is easy to generate. Evidence that the homework happened is harder to fake.See `projectization.build-vs-reuse@0.2.0` in the public PAM repository.
