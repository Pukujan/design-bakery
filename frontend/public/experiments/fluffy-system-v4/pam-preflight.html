The market tension

AI can accelerate implementation faster than a team can improve its planning habits.

Stack Overflow's 2025 developer survey found 84% of respondents use or plan to use AI tools in development, while 69% said they do not plan to use AI for project planning. PAM is aimed at that seam between research and implementation.
