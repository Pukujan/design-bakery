02 · The research desk

Give the agent somewhere to think before you give it somewhere to publish.

Buyer

Who hits the pain first?

Actual job, trigger, frustration, desired progress. Community language beats an invented persona.

Market

What do they use today?

Direct products, spreadsheets, prompts, consultants, internal habits, doing nothing.

Proof

What earns belief?

A product incident, one useful statistic, one research finding, a demo that changes state.

Brand

What should this feel like?

References become a direction: editorial, warm companion, scientific instrument, cinematic 3D, or something else.

The page is the last step.

04 · Why image generation belongs here

A brand can have atmosphere, characters and editorial art. HTML does not have to carry every emotional job by itself.

Image generation

The hero scene on this page was generated for this V3 experiment, then cropped and compressed for the actual layout. Its job is emotional context: late-night product work, research material, an AI companion in the room.

HTML + SVG

The argument, sources, navigation, typography and product explanation stay in code. That keeps the page responsive and lets the generated art do one job well instead of turning into a screenshot of the entire website.

The owner still edits the story

More generation is easy. Better context is the product.

Fluffy gives the agent a repeatable way to research the buyer, market and brand before it spends its creative budget.
