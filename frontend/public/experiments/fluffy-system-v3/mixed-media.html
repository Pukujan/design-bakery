01 · Give each medium a job

Visual richness works when the roles stay separate.

Generated image

Creates atmosphere and human context quickly. It can make a technical product feel lived-in instead of diagrammed from a distance.

I

02 · The mixed-media model

The page still needs one argument. Media should orbit the argument, not compete with it.

One commercial spine

A repo gives the agent product truth. Buyer research, market alternatives, proof and brand research add the context needed for a useful marketing story.

01

Image for feeling

Do not bake the whole message into generated artwork. Keep the copy and CTA in HTML.

02

Vector for meaning

Use lines, gauges, routes and custom symbols when relationships need to stay legible at every screen size.

03

3D for space

If depth does not add information, use 2D. The page should still work when WebGL fails.

04

Typography for the argument

The headline still has to sell the product when every visual asset is removed.

V3 conclusion

More media can make a brand richer. It can also hide weak positioning faster.

That is why Fluffy's media choice sits after buyer research, market framing and brand direction. The renderers get freedom once the story has earned it.
