01 · Separate questions

A single score hides which kind of confidence you actually earned.

Source suitability

Is this the kind of source the claim should rely on? Reputation and suitability are related, but they are not identical questions.

Retrieval quality

Did the system surface the relevant material under the frozen corpus, query and model/config identities?

Temporal validity

Was the evidence current for the point in time the conclusion is supposed to describe?

Semantic support

Does the evidence actually support the claim? In the current program this remains a distinct layer and is not silently inferred from provenance or rank.

03 · Why this visual direction

The brand behaves like a lab instrument because the product is about measurement boundaries.

Custom SVG

Gauges, needles, status rings and measurement paths are crisp, responsive and specific to the product. They can become a real icon and motion system later.

Restrained motion

Needles can move when a dimension changes. Motion explains state rather than decorating an assurance dashboard with ambient noise.

The commercial frame

Know what was tested together. Know what each test established.

That is a clearer sell than “AI assurance platform.” It gives the buyer a concrete reason to care about stack identity, independent checks and explicit unknowns.
