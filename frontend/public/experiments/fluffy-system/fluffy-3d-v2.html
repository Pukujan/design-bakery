Fluffy gives the generator four kinds of context that rarely live in the repo.

Buyer

Who feels the problem?

What happened before they started looking? What do they call the problem in their own words?

Market

What are they doing today?

Direct tools, manual workflows, agencies, spreadsheets, ChatGPT, or staying with the status quo.

Proof

Why should they believe it matters?

Real product incidents, measured outcomes, credible external research, and numbers with context.

Brand

What world should the product belong to?

Live references, emotional territory, typography, motion, product demo, and the visual ideas to avoid.

A rewrite should get more concrete as the context improves.
