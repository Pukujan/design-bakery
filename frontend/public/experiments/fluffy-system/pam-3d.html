Projectization as a launch sequence

Clear the gates before launch.

AI can get a project moving before the team has finished deciding what the project is. PAM gives the research-to-build transition a preflight: scope, reuse, evidence, validation, and continuity before momentum becomes architecture.

The sequence

A project should not become “ready” because an agent produced enough files.

PAM's modules are meant to surface the work that applies to this project, then ask for closure evidence that is harder to fake than a persuasive paragraph.

GATE 01Scope

Freeze the current outcome, non-goals, subsystem boundaries, and rules for what can enter the horizon.

GATE 02Reuse

Search concrete internal and external alternatives before authorizing bespoke work.

GATE 03Evidence

Say what will count when a requirement is marked satisfied. Prefer tests, receipts, exact revisions, and reviewed decisions.

GATE 04Continuity

Give the next human or agent exact resumable state instead of another archaeology session through old chats.

Why one gate was hardened

A real adopter showed that “research alternatives” could pass without enough actual research.

The position

The point is not to slow down the launch. It is to know what cleared it.

PAM is pre-v1 and intentionally bounded. It does not prove the research is true or replace human judgment. It is trying to make the handoff from research to engineering explicit, versioned, and evidence-backed enough that speed does not erase the reasons underneath the project.
