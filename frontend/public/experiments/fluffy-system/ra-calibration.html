A calibration system for AI research

Trust is a set of measurements, not a badge.

A result can come from a good source, have a valid citation, rank highly in retrieval, and still fail a different question. Research Assurance is being built to keep those questions separate, pin the exact stack that answered them, and preserve what was actually established.

The mistake

One green light is easy to overread.

Research systems often produce outputs that look finished because one layer did its job. The product thesis here is that different assurance dimensions should remain independently visible.

The product position

Research Assurance is not trying to become the one evaluator that answers every question.

It is being built as the control plane that knows which independently versioned component answered which assurance question, which exact revisions were tested together, and what evidence supports calling that stack revision usable.

Keep layers independent

Provenance integrity does not imply retrieval quality. Retrieval does not imply truth. Temporal validity does not imply semantic entailment.

Keep experiments identifiable

A result should not be silently reused after the corpus, query set, configuration, temporal policy, or producer revision changes.

The pitch

Know what was tested. Know what passed. Know what still hasn't been proven.

That is a narrower promise than “trustworthy AI.” It is also a more useful one. Research Assurance is an active private research project exploring how independently versioned evidence, retrieval, source, temporal, and future semantic-assurance systems can compose without erasing their boundaries.
