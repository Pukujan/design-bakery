The category problem

“Evaluation” is already several different products wearing the same word.

Source suitability, retrieval quality, citation integrity, temporal validity, semantic support, benchmark integrity, and experiment identity are related. They are not interchangeable.

LAYER 01Evidence identity

Exact source and artifact identity, durable citations, provenance, and replayable lineage.

LAYER 02Retrieval quality

Whether an eligible retriever actually finds and ranks useful evidence under a frozen evaluation condition.

LAYER 03Source suitability

Whether a source is appropriate for this claim and policy context, independent of retrieval rank.

LAYER 04Temporal validity

Whether evidence is valid for the date and time frame of the question being answered.

LAYER 05Semantic support

Whether the evidence actually supports the atomic claim. A future layer can own this without rewriting the others.

LAYER 06Stack compatibility

Which exact revisions and policies were tested together, and whether a result still belongs to the current stack.

The boundary

A control plane should make uncertainty clearer, not hide it.

Research Assurance is still active research. It should not be sold as a finished end-to-end truth machine. The interesting market position is narrower: coordinate independently versioned assurance systems so a team knows what each layer established, which exact stack was evaluated, and where the evidence still stops.
