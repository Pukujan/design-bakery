Generation is crowded. Judgment is where the page gets interesting.

Framer, Figma, Replit, Claude, coding agents, agencies, and designers can all help create strong web experiences. Fluffy is aimed at the work that tells those tools what commercial story to express.

The buyer is part of the input. So are the status quo, the market gap, credible numbers, and the visual references that shape the brand.

What the finished page should know that the repo alone cannot tell it.

Buyer momentWhat happened ten minutes before somebody started looking for this product?
Competitive alternativeWhat would they use if this product disappeared tomorrow?
Reason to moveWhich frustration is large enough to make the current workaround feel expensive?
ProofWhich product incident, customer result, benchmark, or external market fact makes the story believable?
Visual territoryWhich cultural and design references make this product feel distinct before anyone reads the footer?

The voice gets its own edit.

Fluffy's copy pass searches for the familiar structural tells: repeated contrast formulas, perfectly balanced triples, generic abstractions, uniform paragraph shapes, and a slogan at the end of every section.

The rewrite adds concrete product nouns and buyer situations. It does not fake humanity with slang or deliberate mistakes.
