Provenance is not entailment.

The bytes can be intact. The retriever can be good. The model can still write a claim the passage does not support, from a stack that is not the one you benchmarked, against a corpus that has since moved. Specialized systems make scoped decisions. None of them get to promote their own output into truth.

What the product is

An integration and assurance control plane for independently versioned research systems. It pins known-good revisions, defines stack contracts, and keeps the receipt for why a particular composition was called usable.

What it refuses

A universal verified=true. Source suitability, retrieval quality, temporal validity, numeric consistency, semantic support, and downstream correctness stay distinct dimensions.

A result only belongs to the stack that produced it.

The first domain slice is financial intelligence on real SEC-like evidence. Claim verification is not pretended into existence. Retrieval benchmarking stays a research and CI dependency, not a per-request production service. Public protocol numbers describe campaign scope, not a winner.
