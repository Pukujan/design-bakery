What changed

PAM versioned the method instead of pretending the first version had always been stricter.

Search

Preserve traceable internal and external discovery surfaces.

Name

Serious candidates need concrete identities, not categories like “some OSS.”

Test

When a cheap spike can resolve material uncertainty, prefer evidence over prose.

Decide

Build-new has to name the unmet requirements or unacceptable tradeoffs that remain.

The market gap

Most project systems track what you decided to do. PAM is interested in whether the project did the homework that made the decision defensible.

That makes it closer to a versioned project-preflight methodology than another backlog, agent harness, or approval dashboard.

Not a universal stamp

A satisfied PAM requirement does not mean the research conclusion is true, the code is correct, or the release is safe in every domain.

Not human replacement

Humans still own disputed scope, consequential waivers, and final acceptance when judgment cannot be reduced to a deterministic oracle.

The pitch

A persuasive plan should not be allowed to cite itself as proof.

PAM is trying to make projectization repeatable enough that a future agent can see what was researched, which obligations applied, what evidence closed them, and where a human actually made the call.
