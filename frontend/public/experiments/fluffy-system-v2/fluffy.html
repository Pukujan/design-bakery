02 · The missing context

A product repo tells you what exists. Marketing needs the world around it.

Buyer language

“I can get an agent to build the site. I still rewrite the whole launch because it sounds like everybody else.”

Status quo

Direct prompting. Generic website generators. Founder rewrites. Design agents. Agencies. Existing brand systems.

Market evidence

AI content volume is rising. Perceived AI authorship can reduce authenticity in emotional marketing contexts. The burden shifts toward better research and editing.

Brand territory

Study direct competitors and adjacent brands. Analyze first-screen promise, density, typography, product visualization, motion, warmth, trust, and what the category already overuses.

04 · Voice

Human copy comes from better observation, then harder editing.

Start inside the situation.

Use the question, frustration, workaround, or buying trigger a real person would recognize. Product architecture can wait.

Delete the model's favorite shapes.

Repeated contrast formulas, neat three-part benefit stacks, inflated transitions, vague abstractions, fake intimacy, and every section ending with a slogan get another pass.

Keep the useful rough edges.

Specific nouns, uneven sentence lengths, concrete consequences, market language, and direct claims usually sound more credible than perfectly balanced prose.
