Assurance for evidence-heavy AI research

Which part actually passed?

A result can have a reputable source, a clean citation, and a strong retrieval score while another part of the research chain is stale, mismatched, or still untested. Research Assurance keeps those questions separate and binds them to the exact stack that was evaluated.

The product rule
Do not let one assurance layer lend its confidence to another.

Research Assurance coordinates the stack and preserves what each layer actually established. It does not turn provenance, retrieval, or a citation into a universal truth stamp.

04 · Stack identity

The same benchmark result stops being the same result when the research stack changes.

Research Assurance pins exact component revisions and stack-level contracts. Results should not be silently reused across mismatched source heads, corpora, query/qrel identities, model/config revisions, temporal policies, experiment versions, or parent artifact digests.
