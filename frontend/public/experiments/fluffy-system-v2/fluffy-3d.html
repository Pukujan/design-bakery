02 · Buyer

Put a person inside the brief.

What are they trying to get done? What do they complain about? What do they use today? What happened that made the old way stop feeling good enough? The page starts sounding human when the agent has something human to notice.

03 · Market

Find the gap after looking at the alternatives.

Direct competitors are only part of the picture. Status quo, spreadsheets, agencies, internal playbooks, general AI, and “do nothing” all compete for the same job. Fluffy researches the language already flooding the category before it chooses a position.

AI content is already normal.

Ahrefs reported 87% AI-assisted content use among 879 surveyed marketers, with AI users publishing 42% more content per month in that sample.

Ahrefs · 2025 ↗

Volume raises the bar for distinctiveness.

The strategic opportunity shifts upstream: better audience research, clearer competitive framing, better proof selection, and a visual world that belongs to the product.

04 · Proof

Give every strong sentence a reason to exist.

Product evidence proves product behavior. External research explains the market or mechanism. A user quote explains an experience. Fluffy keeps those jobs separate so the marketing can be bold without borrowing certainty it has not earned.

Now decide what the idea should feel like.
