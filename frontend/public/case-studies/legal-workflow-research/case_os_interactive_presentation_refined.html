The real problem

Daily litigation work is fragmented

A simple task often requires checking filings, orders, emails, calendars, rules, notes, prior appearances, and document repositories.

The bottleneck is not one task. It is the constant switching between systems and memory.

One task becomes many checks

Why current tools fall short

Search, chat, and calendars do not solve the full workflow

A legal team needs more than answers. They need task context, source checking, draft preparation, rule awareness, and a work log.

The system should help complete the work, not just answer questions.

Current workflow

  • Staff searches manually
  • Rules checked in separate places
  • Drafts built from scratch
  • Follow-ups tracked manually
  • Knowledge stays in memory

Case OS workflow

  • AI surfaces relevant files
  • Court and part rules are attached to tasks
  • Editable drafts are prepared
  • Next actions are guided
  • Work log captures what happened

Court and case intelligence

Case OS learns from rules, orders, and prior case activity

The system can help staff understand procedural patterns based on court rules, judge behavior, prior appearances, and internal history.

The app gives practical procedural context, but leaves final judgment to legal professionals.

Engineering reliability

Built like a legal AI workflow system, not a chatbot

Behind the simple interface is an engineering layer for safer, testable, reviewable AI workflows.
