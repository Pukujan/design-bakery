The market problem

These can all look like the same wrong answer.

Concept gapThe idea itself is missing.
Syntax gapThe reasoning does not survive translation into code.
Representation gapThe learner understands one form but not another.
State or invariant gapThe moving parts or stable rule are lost.
Source frictionAuthor conventions consume attention.
Tutor frictionThe explanation creates difficulty the concept did not require.

Different failures need different help.

Study OS

Most adaptive tutors ask what to teach next. Study OS first asks why the learner got stuck.

Research support

This problem has roots in learning science and program comprehension.

Representation translation matters

Multiple-representation research emphasizes that learners must understand how representations relate, not simply receive more of them.

More is not always better

A 2024 meta-analysis found positive average effects alongside substantial heterogeneity and support requirements.

Names affect comprehension

Program-comprehension experiments show identifier naming can materially help or hinder understanding.

The evidence page links the papers and separates external research from Study OS claims.

Better personalization

Your learning profile should remember more than your score.

What seems to work

Pattern recognition, concrete state reasoning, explicit index/value iteration, and dictionary use after reframing.

What still needs proof

Unaided reconstruction, changed-surface transfer, delayed retention, and robustness to unfamiliar author conventions.

Not “visual learner.” Not “72% mastered.” What broke, what helped, and what still needs evidence?
