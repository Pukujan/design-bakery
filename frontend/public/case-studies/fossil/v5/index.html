For technical work that lasts longer than the chat

You remember what you chose. Do you remember why?

A few months ago you researched a hard problem, tried a few approaches, ran tests, read the docs and made the call. The work is still somewhere in your chats, tickets and commits. The reason you made the call is much harder to get back.

The problem

The history is there. The thread through it is not.

You do not make a serious technical decision in one message. You read, test, argue with the result, change your mind and try again. AI helps you move through that work faster. You still make the decision.

Why search is not enough

You can ask an AI to reconstruct the story. That is not the same as having the story.

The missing part

Why you believed it

Which source mattered, which test changed your mind, what you ruled out, and what later changed again.

An AI can give you a convincing answer. The hard question is simpler: is that actually why you made the decision?

This is not a new pain

Developers have been losing rationale for years.

AI did not create the problem. It made it easier to produce a lot more research, experiments and discussion in the same amount of time.

What FOSSIL is for

Come back five months later and ask the question you actually care about.

“Why did I move away from the SQLite prototype?”
The decisionYou moved to the durable event core.

Not because SQLite suddenly became bad, but because the assumptions around the workload and recovery model changed.

What changedA test broke an assumption the prototype depended on.

You can follow that change back to the benchmark and the source material you used at the time.

What you already triedSQLite with WAL, serialized writes and replay patches.

Those attempts are still part of the history. They do not disappear because you chose something else.

What needs another lookOne later conclusion still depends on the old assumption.

FOSSIL can show that the basis changed without pretending the conclusion is automatically false.

The point is not a smarter summary. It is being able to check the path yourself.

What you get

FOSSIL keeps the reasons with the work.

When you forget

Ask why you made the call, what evidence mattered, what you tried first and what changed later.

When someone else takes over

They do not have to guess what past-you meant from a ticket or a commit message. They can inspect the trail.

When an old idea comes back

See whether you already tested it, why it lost, and whether the conditions that killed it are still true.

When the project changes

See what used to be true, what is true now, and which later conclusions may need another look.

Where it fits

FOSSIL does not replace memory, Git, Jira or ADRs.

Those tools are useful. They answer different questions. Memory helps bring back useful context. Git shows what changed. Jira tracks the work. ADRs record a decision. FOSSIL keeps the evidence and reasoning that connect those pieces over time.

Git can tell you how the code changed. FOSSIL helps you remember why your thinking changed.

The pitch

Keep the why.

You already did the research. You already ran the tests. You already made the decision. FOSSIL makes sure that months from now, you do not have to invent the story again.
