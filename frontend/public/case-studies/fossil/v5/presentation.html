The decision is easy to record. The path to it is what gets lost.

What survives

Almost everything is saved. Just not in a form you can trust later.

Chats

Git and tickets

The missing thread

Which evidence changed your mind, what you ruled out and why the final choice made sense then.

The trap

An AI can reconstruct the story later.

It may give you a very good answer. It may even be mostly right.

But how do you know that is the reason you actually used?

Generated rationale and preserved history are not the same thing.

What FOSSIL does

Keep the why while you are doing the work.

What convinced you

Sources, evidence, benchmarks and observations stay connected to the decision.

What you tried

Rejected approaches and old conclusions remain part of the history.

Where it fits

This is not a replacement for memory, Git, Jira or ADRs.

Those tools keep useful pieces

Memory brings back context. Git shows code changes. Jira tracks work. ADRs record decisions.

FOSSIL keeps the thread

Why you believed something, what changed your mind, what came before and what still depends on it.

Git tells you how the code changed. FOSSIL helps you remember why your thinking changed.
