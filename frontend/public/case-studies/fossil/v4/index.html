The actual market gap

Modern work saves the artifacts. It does not save what those artifacts established.

The problem is not that your work disappeared. The problem is that no system turns the research trail into an authoritative history of why your technical position changed.

What you have

Transcripts, commits, tickets, docs

The raw material is usually there: chat history, source links, issue comments, benchmark output, PRs, notes and code.

What is missing

The reasoning chain

Which source mattered? Which assumption changed? Which alternative failed? Which decision depended on the old belief?

What AI can do later

Reconstruct a plausible story

That story may be useful. It may even be mostly right. But can it prove it is the same reasoning path you actually followed?

FOSSIL sells the missing layer: a durable record of why human-led technical decisions became justified.

The workflow changed

One engineer can now generate a month of decision context in an afternoon.

Not because the AI owns the work. Because the human can explore faster: ask more questions, compare more systems, run more experiments, and revise more assumptions.

Product promise

FOSSIL keeps the reasoning while the work is happening.

It is not trying to replace your AI, your project management tool, or your architecture records. It preserves the chain those systems leave behind.

For the person doing the work

Stop rediscovering your own thinking. Ask why you made a decision months ago, what changed later, and what alternatives you already tried.

For the team inheriting the work

Keep institutional knowledge inspectable. New people can see the evidence path behind a decision, not just the final ADR or a model's summary.

For AI-assisted engineering

Give the model a record to inspect. AI can still help answer questions, but FOSSIL gives it a preserved history rather than a pile of fragments.

For serious technical decisions

Know what is current, historical, disputed or stale. FOSSIL keeps what changed over time instead of flattening the project into one latest answer.

Positioning

The category is not “better memory.” It is decision-lineage infrastructure.

Adjacent tools already matter

  • Memory systems retrieve useful prior context.
  • Search finds old artifacts.
  • ADR tools record final decisions.
  • Jira/Git/Slack preserve pieces of the work.

The FOSSIL gap

  • Why this decision? Evidence, sources, tests and alternatives.
  • What changed? Superseded assumptions and later revisions.
  • What was tried before? Historical attempts remain queryable.
  • Can we trust this answer? It is grounded in preserved lineage, not just reconstruction.
