The market gap

After the fact, AI can explain. But can it prove?

A model can read old transcripts, Jira tickets and logs, then produce a confident answer to “why did we choose this?”

Good for recall

“You probably chose Postgres for concurrency, reliability and team familiarity.”

Not enough for provenance

Was that the actual chain? Which source mattered? Which benchmark changed your mind? What did it supersede?
