# Multi-Network Character Minds: LLM Orchestration, Affective Development, and the Open Gap

## A Landscape Survey and Dual-Brain Design Thesis

### Position and Survey Paper

## Abstract

We survey systems that aim to produce a single coherent AI character by combining more than one computational module, especially specialized neural networks, under a large language model (LLM) that acts as slow, deliberative control. We distinguish four architectural patterns: (A) single-LLM "prompt-modular" brains; (B) LLM as orchestrator over trained modality networks; (C) multi-network stacks without an LLM controller; and (D) hybrid LLM + world-model / action stacks. Across academic agents (Generative Agents, Voyager, CAMEL), surveys of LLM agents and Agent AI, and industry stacks (NVIDIA ACE, Inworld, Convai, Soul Machines), we find that production multi-network character pipelines exist but remain closed, while open research largely stays in pattern A. We separately review affective development (emotion as a process over time, not one-shot recognition), self-directed learning, and self-model / metacognition literature, and argue that no open system yet unifies persistent affective growth, multi-modal embodiment, and fail-closed safety layering as a single character substrate. We state a dual-brain design thesis, LLM as prefrontal-style reasoning; specialized nets for fast affect, perception, and motor control, and map open research problems without claiming a completed implementation of the full stack.

No original experimental results are reported.

---

# 1. Introduction

Character AI products and research agents increasingly look "alive" in dialogue, yet most believable behavior still rides a single foundation model with long prompts and memory retrieval. A different design goal treats the LLM as one organ among many: a slow controller that reasons, plans, and narrates, while separate networks handle embedding, vision, affect dynamics, speech, and motion. That split mirrors a crude cognitive analogy, deliberation versus fast specialized subsystems, and raises an empirical question: has anyone shipped that full stack as an open, integrated mind?

This note is a landscape and gap paper. It does not claim consciousness, agency, or product readiness for any private system. It does claim a structural observation: pattern B (LLM + specialized nets) is industrially real and academically underserved as open, reproducible character engines; affective development research is fragmented; self-awareness stacks remain largely theoretical; self-learning that works in practice is mostly LLM + environment feedback + skill libraries, not classical curiosity modules alone.

# 4. Background: Why Multi-Network Characters

Single-LLM characters scale with context length and retrieval quality, but they force every function (affect, vision, motor intent, self-critique) through the same generative path. Multi-network designs trade integration cost for: (1) specialized accuracy and latency on modalities; (2) continuous-time dynamics that do not require a token for every tick; (3) independent training and safety gates per subsystem. The cost is interface design: state schemas, arbitration when modules disagree, and audit of which module drove which action.

# 5. Architectural Patterns

## 5.1 Pattern A: LLM as Brain (Prompt-Modular)

One LLM; modules are prompts and tools (memory stream, reflection, planning). Exemplars: Generative Agents / Smallville (Park et al., arXiv:2304.03442); Voyager (Wang et al., arXiv:2305.16291); CAMEL (Li et al., arXiv:2303.17760); mass-market Character.AI-style products. Strength: iteration speed. Weakness: affect and perception remain linguistic unless external sensors are bolted on as tools.

## 5.2 Pattern B: LLM Orchestrator + Specialized Networks

LLM decides and narrates; separate trained models do ASR/TTS, face animation, body motion, sometimes emotion recognition. Public exemplars: NVIDIA ACE (Riva + LLM + Audio2Face + Omniverse), Inworld, Convai, Soul Machines' modular "Digital Brain" framing. Academic near-match: multi-agent emotion response pipelines that pair CNN emotion recognition with policy, content, and safety agents. Strength: best-of-breed modalities. Weakness: closed stacks; scarce open full engines.

## 5.3 Pattern C: Fully Multi-Network Without LLM Control

Classic game AI and animation nets without a foundation-model controller. Strong on latency and determinism; weak on open-ended dialogue and long-horizon personality.

## 5.4 Pattern D: Hybrid LLM + World Model / VLA

LLM (or VLA stack) grounded via vision-language-action adapters and embodied benchmarks (e.g. ManiSkill, BEHAVIOR-line work). Closer to robots than to social characters with persistent affect, but shares the multi-network integration problem.

# 6. Related Work by Strand

## 6.1 LLM Agents and Surveys

Wang et al. (arXiv:2308.11432) survey LLM-based autonomous agents and a unified construction framing (profiling, memory, planning, action). Masterman et al. (arXiv:2404.11584) map single- vs multi-agent systems for reasoning, planning, and tool calling. Durante et al. (arXiv:2401.03568) define Agent AI as multimodal perception-reasoning-action systems and argue environmental grounding reduces hallucination. These works establish the field vocabulary; they do not deliver an open multi-network *character* reference implementation.

## 6.2 Embodied Skill Learning

Voyager demonstrates lifelong skill acquisition in Minecraft via automatic curriculum, code skill library, and self-verification, working in practice, pattern A/D hybrid. MetaGPT (arXiv:2308.00352) and LATS (arXiv:2310.04406) show multi-agent SOPs and tree search with self-reflection for problem solving. Open-ended real-world problem discovery without human-shaped rewards remains largely unsolved.

## 6.3 Affective Development (Not Only Recognition)

Recognition-focused affective computing is mature relative to *development*. Emergence and process strands include: prediction-error / intrinsic-motivation emotion models (e.g. Schillaci et al., arXiv:2007.14632); free-energy style emotional dynamics from scene features (Ushio et al., IEEE Access line, 2026); MLLM affective geometry aligned with neural data (Du et al., project page 2025-era); personality + appraisal + memory for robots that change with history (Tang et al., arXiv:2503.15518); lifelong empathic motion generation (L2-EMG, arXiv:2512.19551); PsychoAgent-style appraisal process simulation (CogSci 2026 line). The literature does not share one definition of "emotion," lacks long-horizon longitudinal open benchmarks, and rarely integrates continuous affect with multi-modal embodiment and dialogue safety.

## 6.4 Self-Models and Metacognition

Conceptual architectures for autonomous cognitive entities and recursive self-improvement (e.g. ACE-style layered frameworks, arXiv:2310.06775) outpace open running systems that maintain a durable self-model and revise it under fail-closed policy. Metacognitive monitoring (confidence, self-critique loops) is common in agents; developmental self-consciousness networks are not an established engineering product class.

## 6.5 Industry Multi-Modal Characters

NVIDIA ACE is the clearest public modular pipeline of separate trained services. Inworld and Convai package LLM + voice + animation for games. Soul Machines market modular "Digital People." These validate pattern B commercially; they do not yield a single open standard for character state (affect, relationship, goals) across vendors.

# 7. Gap Analysis

1. **No open full pattern-B character engine** combining dialogue LLM, trained affect dynamics (not only prompts), emotional TTS, face/body nets, and persistent state under one inspectable orchestration contract.
2. **No shared multi-modal character benchmark** covering dialogue quality, affective consistency over long time, animation fidelity, latency, and personality stability (agent benchmarks exist; character-complete suites do not).
3. **No interoperable character-state schema** for mood, relationships, and goals across systems.
4. **Emotion-as-prompt remains dominant** in open agent demos; dedicated affect networks stay narrow or proprietary.
5. **Self-consciousness / durable self-model** is not a shipped open module class; metacognition is not selfhood.
6. **Safety composition** for multi-layer character systems (when affect, social learning, or dread-like modules may influence output) is under-specified relative to dialogue-only moderation.

---

# 8. Design Thesis: Dual-Brain Character Architecture

We record a design thesis for future work (not an evaluation result): treat the LLM as the slow reasoning organ (planning, language, tool policy) and run specialized subsystems for fast or continuous functions, local embedding, vision, affect dynamics (e.g. homeostatic and Hebbian-style growth where used), speech I/O, and motion. A training/ops manager coordinates data, evals, and freezes. Optional staged layers (social learning, neural mood shadow, higher-risk modules) sit behind explicit fail-closed gates so capability is not silently on.

This thesis is closer to pattern B with an explicit continuous-time affect path than to Generative Agents' pattern A. It is also closer to neuroscience metaphor than most agent papers claim; the metaphor is scaffolding for system boundaries, not a proof of biological equivalence.

---

# 9. Implications for Builders

- Prefer explicit interfaces between orchestrator and modules over expanding system prompts alone when continuous state matters.
- Separate *recognition* of human emotion from *development* of agent affect; cite which strand a module implements.
- For self-learning, ship environment feedback + skill libraries + verification before speculative recursive self-improvement kernels.
- Publish character-state schemas early if interoperability matters.
- Keep high-risk layers gated; shadow/observer modes before write authority.

---

# 11. Conclusion

Multi-network character AI is real in industry and incomplete in the open. Academic agents prove social simulacra and skill growth with LLM-centric designs. Affective development research is genuine but fragmented. Self-models remain thin. The dual-brain thesis, LLM as slow control plus specialized continuous modules, with fail-closed staging, is a coherent research program, not a solved product class. Closing the open pattern-B gap, standardizing character state, and evaluating long-horizon affect are concrete next scientific targets.
